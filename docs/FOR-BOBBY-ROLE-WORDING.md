# Role model: wording for the CDI Procedure

*For Bobby. OrgOS already applies this as a labelled amendment (A1) on the Document Standards page. When the Procedure is reissued with it, OrgOS drops the amendment on its own.*

**Annex C (control fields).** Remove the rows "Accountable role" and "Executing role or system". Add one row:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Control owner | The one role answerable for this control, stated in the control itself. It must be a role that exists in Org Roles. |

**V08.** Replace "accountable and executing roles" with "a Control owner (one role)".

**V07 (role check).** Every role named in a control, on the cover (Document owner, first-pass and final approval) must exist in Org Roles, by its name or an agreed alias. A field name ("Control Owner", "Document Owner", "Custodian Role") is not a role. Where a role does not exist, the author may use a suggested existing role (the document is changed to its official name), choose another, or create the role in Org Roles.

**7.8.** Roles are named by their Org Roles title, one role per control.

**Control owner (definition).** The role that must see to it that the control operates and that its evidence is produced. One per control.
