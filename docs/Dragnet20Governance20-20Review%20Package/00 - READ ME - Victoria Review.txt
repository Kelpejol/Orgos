DRAGNET RECORDS GOVERNANCE FAMILY - VICTORIA REVIEW

This package contains the proposed successor CDI/records-governance family and the current rationalised records working set.

Order:
01 CDI Policy
02 CDI Procedure
03 Information & Records Governance Standard
04 Records Taxonomy & Metadata Specification
05 Overview, Transition and Retirement Note
06 Readiness & Transition Log
07 Enterprise Record Type Taxonomy
08 Enterprise Records Catalogue

Important distinction:
- 01-04 are durable control drafts intended for formal issue after review/approval.
- 05-06 explain and track the transition; temporary project/readiness matters belong there, not inside the permanent control text.
- 07-08 are rationalised working governance records. They provide the baseline for validation but are not represented as a complete manual review of the entire estate. Ongoing GRC-supported regularisation is part of the intended operating model.

Core drafting principle:
Authoritative reference first; governed extension where necessary.
